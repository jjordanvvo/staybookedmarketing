What drives the price

  • Niche competitiveness: cost-per-lead in personal injury or real estate runs higher than in most home services, so campaigns cost more to fill the same calendar.
  • Ad spend level: media budget scales the volume; the service layer prices the system.
  • Deliverables: ads-only costs less than ads plus landing pages, creative, follow-up automation, and booking systems.
  • Who actually does the work: senior operators cost more than junior account managers running templates.

The honest fine print

Cheaper than that usually means one of three things: an intern running your budget, no follow-up system (so leads leak away between 5pm and 9am), or a contract that locks you in regardless of results. Ask any agency you evaluate the same three questions: how fast do you contact a new lead, who answers on weekends, and what happens if you deliver nothing. The answers matter more than the retainer number.
