What drives the price

  • How competitive your industry is: cost per lead in personal injury or real estate runs higher than in most home services.
  • Ad spend: the media budget sets the volume; the service fee pays for the system.
  • What's included: ads alone cost less than ads plus landing pages, creative, follow-up automation, and booking.
  • Who does the work: senior people cost more than junior account managers running templates.

How much does a marketing agency cost per month?

Is ad spend included in the agency retainer?
Usually not: most agencies bill media spend separately from their service fee, and some mark it up. Stay Booked Marketing has the ad platforms bill you directly, so you always know what bought media and what paid for the service.
How much should a small business spend on advertising per month?
A common guideline is 5 to 10 percent of revenue. For local lead generation, a few thousand dollars a month in ad spend is enough to produce a steady flow of leads in most industries; fast follow-up is what turns them into booked appointments.
